        Reading the card: ADP is overall consensus draft position; Pos ADP Rank is where that puts them among others at their position. When the projection rank (the big number) sits ahead of the Pos ADP Rank, that's a player being drafted later than their projection suggests — a target. Health and Trend grades are carried through as provided in the source projections file.

        SOS is each player's team's 2026 strength of schedule (1 = easiest schedule in the league, 32 = hardest), sourced from 4for4's schedule-release analysis to stay consistent with the projections. SOS 1–5 is the 5 easiest schedules, SOS 28–32 is the 5 hardest, everything between shows SOS 6–27. It's a team-level rating, not split by position, and different sites publish different SOS methodologies that can disagree — treat it as one more data point, not gospel.

        Mine marks a player on your roster; Taken marks a player gone to another team. Both sync to this page for anyone viewing it. Once either is set, check Keeper for a player who was already on that roster from a prior season — keepers stay marked as owned but are left out of the Round Tracker's pick count, so year-to-year keepers across every team don't throw off the round/pick math as this year's picks come off the board.
